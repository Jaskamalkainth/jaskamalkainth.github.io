    Things in the room

    This is a hand-built model of the corner where I write this site: an L-shaped desk, two curved monitors, a laptop and a third screen, a shelf of trinkets, posters and two whiteboards. It follows your local time of day, so the light through the window changes, and the clock and laptop show your time. Each object opens something on the site.

    • Left monitor: everything I've written
    • Right monitor: HNSW, how vector search works
    • Side monitor: interactive experiments
    • Laptop: about me and my day job in search
    • Big whiteboard: Bloom filters
    • Small whiteboard: segment trees and DSU problems
    • Keyboard: C++ notes
    • Dice: Monte Carlo simulation
    • Toy bicycle: Fourier epicycles
    • Posters and headphones: music I love
    • Dead Poets Society poster: philosophy notes
    • Into the Wild poster: why we do what we do
    • 사랑 sign: Korean learning notes
    • Snack box: emotions vs hunger
    • Photo frames: pixelate your image
