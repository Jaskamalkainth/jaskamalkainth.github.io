    Things in the room

    This is a hand-built model of the home office where I write this site. My side has an L-shaped desk with two curved monitors, a laptop and a third screen, a shelf of trinkets, posters and a whiteboard. Across the room there's a second, standing desk, a cabinet of books by the window and a family-tree clock. Orbit all the way round: the wall nearest you fades away like a dollhouse. The room follows your local time of day, so the light through the window changes and the clocks and laptop show your time. Each object opens something on the site.

    • Left monitor: everything I've written
    • Right monitor: HNSW, how vector search works
    • Side monitor: interactive experiments
    • Laptop: about me and my day job in search
    • Whiteboard by the window: Bloom filters
    • Books on the cabinet: philosophy notes
    • Small whiteboard: segment trees and DSU problems
    • Keyboard: C++ notes
    • Dice: Monte Carlo simulation
    • Toy bicycle: Fourier epicycles
    • Posters and headphones: music I love
    • Dead Poets Society poster: philosophy notes
    • Into the Wild poster: why we do what we do
    • 사랑 sign: Korean learning notes
    • Snack box: emotions vs hunger
    • Photo frames: pixelate your image
