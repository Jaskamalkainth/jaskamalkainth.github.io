# Samsung Galaxy Book6 Series: Which Tier Should Students Buy?

Samsung's current Galaxy Book lineup is a single series split into three configurable tiers, which makes it a genuinely useful case study for students trying to figure out how much laptop they actually need: **Galaxy Book6**, **Galaxy Book6 Pro**, and **Galaxy Book6 Ultra**. ## Which Tier Fits Which Student

**Galaxy Book6** — the base tier's 16GB RAM and 512GB storage cover writing, research, browsing, and note-taking comfortably. This is the one to pick if your laptop's job is mostly documents, PDFs, and a browser with too many tabs open.

**Galaxy Book6 Pro** — the sensible middle ground for CS/engineering students: a faster Ultra 7 chip and the option to go up to 32GB RAM / 1TB storage handles heavier IDEs, local dev environments, and running a VM or two without the Ultra tier's price.

**Galaxy Book6 Ultra** — the Ultra X7 chip, 32GB RAM as standard, and a 16.0" touch display option make sense for students doing sustained compile-heavy work, large datasets, or design/video coursework where the extra headroom (and screen real estate) actually gets used.

## The Trade-off to Know Before Buying

None of the three tiers list a discrete GPU in the configuration options above — all rely on integrated graphics. That's fine for coursework, IDEs, and everyday multitasking, but if your program specifically needs GPU-accelerated work (ML training, 3D rendering, certain CAD tools), that's worth checking directly on Samsung's configurator or looking outside this lineup before buying.
