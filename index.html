    What I work on

    Product search at Samsung, live in 55+ markets. A query goes in and ranked products come out. My team owns everything in between.

    1. q

      Query understanding

      Normalise, correct, classify intent, expand. Work out what the person actually meant.

      NLPspell-correctLLMs
    2. R

      Retrieval

      Lexical and semantic, fused. BM25 inverted indexes next to k-NN over HNSW graphs.

      OpenSearchBM25HNSW / Faiss
    3. f(x)

      Ranking

      Ordering candidates by relevance with ML models and signals, tuned by measurement.

      PyTorchrelevance
    4. σ

      Reranking

      GenAI and LLMs over the top of the list, where precision matters most.

      vLLMTGIOllamaMCP
    5. ∞

      Serving

      Spring and FastAPI microservices on AWS, with Redis caching and logging APIs. It has to stay up, globally.

      AWSRedisobservability

    I lead a search engineering team that owns the backend microservices, search and logging APIs, and relevance end to end. The platform started in one market and now runs in more than 55, and it has stayed highly available while search quality kept improving.

    Before that I spent a long time on algorithms: ACM-ICPC, competitive programming, and 300+ hours teaching data structures.
