    What I work on

    Product search at Samsung, live in 55+ markets. A query goes in and ranked products come out. My team owns everything in between.

      Query understanding

      Normalise, correct, classify intent, expand. Work out what the person actually meant.

      Retrieval

      Lexical and semantic, fused. BM25 inverted indexes next to k-NN over HNSW graphs.

      OpenSearchBM25HNSW / Faiss

      Ranking

      Ordering candidates by relevance with ML models and signals, tuned by measurement.

      PyTorchrelevance
    4. σ

      Reranking

      Spring and FastAPI microservices on AWS, with Redis caching and logging APIs. It has to stay up, globally.
